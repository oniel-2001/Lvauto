Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

LV Auto Market is a marketing and lead-generation brand operated in Las Vegas, Nevada. Vehicle sales are completed through Findlay Chevrolet. This page explains what we collect on this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us. That includes your name, phone number, and any vehicle details you provide on the trade-in or buying power pages — such as a VIN, mileage, an estimated payoff range, or the type of vehicle you're shopping for.

The forms on this site do not store your information on this website. When you tap the send button, your device opens a text message addressed to us with your answers filled in. Nothing is transmitted until you personally send that message.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your Social Security number or date of birth on this website.
  • We do not run a credit check from this website, and no page here is a credit application.
  • We do not sell your information to third-party lead brokers or data resellers.

How we use your information

We use what you send to respond to you directly, to look up vehicles that fit what you're asking for, to estimate a trade value, and to coordinate your purchase with Findlay Chevrolet. If you move forward with a purchase, your information will be shared with Findlay Chevrolet and, where financing is involved, with lenders you authorize as part of a formal application.

Text messages and calls

By sending us a text or providing your phone number, you agree that we may contact you by call or text about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts from us.

Third-party services

This website is hosted by a third-party provider and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may log standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type as part of delivering the page.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete the information you sent us at any time by calling or texting (702) 937-5542. Nevada residents may have additional rights regarding the sale of personal information under Nevada law; we do not sell personal information as defined by that law.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call or text (702) 937-5542.

This policy is provided for general informational purposes and is not legal advice. Have an attorney review it before relying on it for your business.
